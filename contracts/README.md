## Related-party note

Both parties share a mailing address, which can read as a conflict of
interest — especially with DTC's 501(c)(3) application pending, where
undisclosed self-dealing between a nonprofit and a business under common
control can jeopardize tax-exempt status or trigger IRS excess-benefit
penalties. You confirmed Lucas Harris has no board seat, officer role, or
other governance position at DTC and the shared address is coincidental,
so no further disclosure should be needed — but the agreement now
includes a "No Affiliation" clause (Section 14.7) documenting that fact
and that the fees reflect arm's-length terms, so the record is protected
either way.
