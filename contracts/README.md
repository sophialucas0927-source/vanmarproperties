# DTC Website Services Agreement — DRAFT

`DTC-Website-Services-Agreement.docx` is a draft services agreement between
**Mission Hub Ink** (a sole proprietorship) and **Dominate Through Christ**
(DTC, dthroughc.org — 77824 Wildcat Dr., Palm Desert, CA 92211), effective
September 11, 2026, covering:

- One-time website development fee: **$500 total, 100% due upfront, paid
  by check**
- Recurring maintenance fee: **$250/month, paid via Stripe**, cancellable
  by the client at any time (effective end of the current billing cycle)

## Before this is signed

This is a **template, not legal advice**. Still open (highlighted in
yellow in the document):

- Mission Hub Ink's owner's full legal name and principal address —
  because it's a **sole proprietorship**, it isn't a separate legal
  entity; the contract is with the individual owner doing business as
  "Mission Hub Ink," and that person is personally liable under the
  agreement (no corporate liability shield). Worth knowing before signing.
- Signatory name/title for DTC

Resolved: DTC's 501(c)(3) application is pending (not yet granted) — the
agreement discloses this and puts responsibility for any tax-deductibility
representations to donors on DTC. Governing law is California, venue
Riverside County, matching DTC's own published site Terms.

Given that DTC is a charitable initiative that publishes content about
minors (sponsored student athletes) and is donation-adjacent (even though
donations are processed by a separate "giving partner," not this website),
and that Mission Hub Ink's owner carries personal liability as a sole
proprietor, **a licensed attorney should review this draft before it is
signed.**
